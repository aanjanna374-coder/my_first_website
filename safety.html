Be ready before it happens

Safety guidance

Simple actions that protect you, your family, and your community.

Before an emergency

01Prepare a kit with water, food, medicines, a torch, batteries, and copies of important documents.
02Save emergency numbers and identify the safest route out of your home or workplace.
03Follow official alerts and keep your phone charged when severe weather is expected.

During an emergency

01Stay calm and follow instructions from local authorities.
02Stay away from flooded roads, fallen wires, damaged buildings, and broken glass.
03Help children, older adults, and people with disabilities move to safety.
04Use text messages when possible to keep phone lines available for urgent calls.
