Know the risks

Learn how to respond before an emergency happens.

Flood

Move to higher ground and never walk or drive through moving water.

Fire

Raise the alarm, leave quickly, and call emergency services from a safe place.

Earthquake

Drop, cover, and hold on. Stay away from windows and damaged buildings.

Cyclone

Secure loose objects and follow official evacuation instructions.
